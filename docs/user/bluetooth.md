# Bluetooth headphones

Settings → Bluetooth shows whether Bluetooth is on and which headphones are connected. Open it to turn Bluetooth on or off and to connect, switch or pair headphones. The player remembers up to six, most recent first.

## Pairing

1. Put the headphones in pairing mode.
2. Select **Pair new device** and press ▶. Found devices appear below it.
3. Select your headphones and press ▶. The row says "Connecting…", then "Connected to …".

Pairing with a PIN or a six-digit code isn't supported.

## Everyday use

- With Bluetooth on, the player connects to the most recent headphones when it starts and whenever they come back in range. A stalled connection is reset and retried within about 15 seconds.
- ▶ on other remembered headphones switches to them. ▶ on the connected ones disconnects them until you press ▶ again or restart. Hold ▶ to forget them.
- Music goes to Bluetooth whenever nothing is plugged into either jack. To switch to wired headphones, pause, plug them in and press play: while audio goes to Bluetooth the player can't see the jack.
- Each play or resume waits about half a second before the music starts, so the headphones don't miss the opening.
- The headphones' controls work: play, pause, next and previous, and in-ear detection pauses. If they disconnect, playback pauses.
- The player sends the level the 3.5 mm jack would get at the same volume; the headphones' own volume is separate.
- With nothing connected for 5 minutes, Bluetooth switches off to save battery. It comes back when you open its screen, press play with nothing plugged in, or restart.

A Bluetooth mark beside the play icon shows Bluetooth on (dim) or connected (bright). LDAC and other codecs beyond the module's own aren't supported.
